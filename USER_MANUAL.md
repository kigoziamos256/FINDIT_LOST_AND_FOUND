## 5. Claiming a found item

1. Open the **found item's** page.
2. Fill in the **📩 Claim this item** form:
   - **Your phone or email for pickup** — required. The finder will use this to reach you *only after* an admin approves the claim.
   - **Message to the finder** — optional. Describe distinguishing features so they know it's yours.
3. Click **Submit claim**. The item's status becomes **CLAIM_PENDING** and the person who found it is notified.

You cannot claim your own found item, and you cannot claim the same item twice.

## 5.1 What happens after approval?

Once an admin approves your claim:

- The item is marked **RESOLVED**
- You receive a notification with the **finder's name and email**, plus the **pickup location** if they set one
- The finder receives a notification with **your name, email, and the contact info you entered**
- Visiting the item page shows a green **🤝 Get in touch** card with all the details both of you need

Coordinate pickup directly using those details. FindIt does not arrange physical handover — that's the last step between you and the finder.
